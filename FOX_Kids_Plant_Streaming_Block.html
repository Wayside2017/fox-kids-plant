About FOX Kids Plant

FOX Kids Plant is an independent, fan-made Canadian children's television project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by FOX, Fox Kids, or the producers, broadcasters, and rights holders of the programmes listed. Show titles and trademarks belong to their respective owners. The catalogue is a curated starter collection, not a claim to list every Canadian children's programme ever made. Some entries are international co-productions with Canadian production involvement. Provider links lead to The Roku Channel, Tubi, and Prime Video. Availability must be checked on each service; FOX Kids Plant does not host or redistribute their videos.
